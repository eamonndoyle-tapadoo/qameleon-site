How it works

Here is our board. Here is our git. Here is our product.

Three connections, then QAmeleon gets to work in three stages. Your developer sets it up alone, from the command line.

1

Build

QAmeleon signs in to your dev app, walks every page, records your API and proposes the checks. You approve a baseline, and the morning and deploy runs start.

2

Review

It works through every ticket in your QA column, step by step, on the real dev app, and writes up what it saw: what passed, what failed, what it could not check and why.

3

Refine

Every check that passes and can be automated joins the morning run. Every bug pattern it finds goes on its checklist. Coverage grows with each review.

Safety

It writes on your board. So it has to be careful.

Trust is the product. These rules are built in, and you can add to them but not switch them off.

  • Your CI, your keysIt runs in your own pipeline on your own Anthropic key. No ticket text or page content reaches us.
  • Dev only, behind a fenceEvery write is blocked unless a test allows it. Publish, send, invite, checkout and billing are always blocked.
  • Its own data, cleaned upTests make clearly named throwaway records, delete them, and report "nothing left behind" every run.
  • No spend without a yesAnything that costs money in your app waits for a person to say go.
  • Some things wait for a personSecurity findings, critical findings, and anything odd in a ticket are held for review, never posted on their own.
  • Tickets are data, not ordersIf a ticket tries to tell the reviewer what to do, it reports that as a finding and does nothing else.
  • Secrets never leakEvery report is scanned for anything credential-like before it is posted. If one is found, nothing goes out.
  • A pause buttonOne command stops all publishing at once. If triage starts rejecting findings, it falls back to asking first.

Setup

First morning report in about half an hour.

Every question has a detected default. Passwords go in through hidden prompts, never a file. Nothing is posted until you say so.

Reviews get sharper with a few test accounts and some realistic data. qameleon doctor tells you what to add next.

$ npx qameleon init Dev URL found, sign-in form detected, timezone Europe/Dublin $ qameleon accounts add $ qameleon discover Proposed: pages, endpoints, cross-account checks $ qameleon run --dry-run $ qameleon connect Board, chat and CI connected. Morning runs on. $ qameleon review
